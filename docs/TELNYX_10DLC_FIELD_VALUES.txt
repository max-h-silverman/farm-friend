CONTENT DETAILS

Campaign Description
Vashon Island Growers Association (VIGA) operates VIGA Farm Friend to keep the VIGA farm-stand map fresh and useful. VIGA Farm Friend sends operational SMS messages for participating Vashon farmers and community members. Donations are not solicited.

Opt In Workflow Description (Message Flow/Call To Action)
Farmers opt in while completing VIGA Farm Friend onboarding: they accept the SMS agreement on the form, enter their phone number, then text VIGA from that phone to VIGA Farm Friend. That inbound text confirms control of the phone and completes setup. Farm Friend then sends operational farm-stand messages, including update reminders, publication confirmations, and customer stock-out reports. Community members may opt in by texting JOIN after viewing the call to action at https://www.vigavashon.org/viga-farm-friend. START remains available to restart messaging after an opt-out.

AUTO-RESPONSES

Opt in message

Opt out message
VIGA Farm Friend: You have been unsubscribed and will no longer receive messages from us. Reply HELP for assistance.

Help message
VIGA Farm Friend: For help, reply HELP or contact us at board@vigavashon.org. Msg frequency varies. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.
